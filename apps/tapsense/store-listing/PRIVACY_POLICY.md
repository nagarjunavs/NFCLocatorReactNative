# TapSense privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

TapSense helps you find your phone's NFC antenna and confirm the tap area with a real tap test.

## What the app reads
- Device model information (manufacturer, model and, where available, SKU). It is used on the device
  to look up the antenna position in a catalog bundled with the app.
- NFC hardware state (on or off) and whether a tag or reader touched the phone during a tap test. No
  tag contents are read, stored or sent.
- The app does not access your account, contacts, location, photos or payment data.

## What leaves the device
Nothing. TapSense has no account system, no analytics or advertising SDK and makes no network requests
of its own. The Rate row opens the store's review prompt, which is handled by Google Play or Apple.
The Contact support row opens a draft in your mail app; nothing is sent unless you send it.

## What is stored on the device
Your preferences (a chosen phone, haptics, reduced motion, appearance and whether onboarding is
finished) and a small catalog cache, kept in the app's private storage. Deleting the app removes
them.
